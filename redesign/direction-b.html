Hearing protection · in development

Turn down the sounds that bother you. Keep the rest.

Earplugs lower every frequency at once. Haven seals your ear, then plays the room back with only the bands you choose softened. Try it on the chart.

01 · Why

The loud places worth going to are the ones that hurt.

A foam plug lowers everything evenly. Voices get muffled, music loses its detail, and people take them out. Typical levels in places people go every week:

100+Concerts, clubsOften over 100 dB near the stage. You want the band clear, not the harsh peaks.
95Power tools, kitchensNoise mixed with conversation you still need to follow.
90Subways, stadiumsLong exposure where a flat plug makes you miss announcements.
02 · How it works

A sealed earbud with a filter you control.

Four steps, all inside the earbud. The model below is the real v2 design at true scale. Turn it, pull it apart, or follow the sound path.

Step 1Seal

A foam tip blocks most outside sound, powered or not.

Step 2Listen

An outward-facing mic picks up the room.

Step 3Soften

Up to five bands are turned down, then a ceiling caps the level.

Step 4Play back

A small speaker fills the sealed ear in about 3 ms, too fast to hear as an echo.

Ear model built from published anatomical dimensions, not a scan.

03 · The app

The chart, in your pocket.

The app is where you set the bands. It sends them to the earbud over Bluetooth, and two independent limits keep the sound below a fixed ceiling.

Home

Protected or paused, shown as one slow amber light.

Tune

Up to five bands, each from a gentle dip to fully removed.

Comfort check

Tones that rise slowly, a stop button always in reach, and an 85 dB cap in both the app and the earbud.

Check-ins

A few taps after a loud day. Over weeks, see which settings worked.

04 · Hardware

What's inside v2.

Design targets from the current v2 plan. None of these are measured on a built earbud yet.

Body16.7 × 18.9 × 17.5 mm · nozzle for standard 4.5 mm-bore tips
AudioADAU1860 codec with on-chip signal processor · five filters, limiter, output ceiling
WirelessnRF5340 Bluetooth LE module · settings only, the audio never leaves the earbud
BatteryRechargeable coin cell · 6+ hour target
ChargingCase with two-pin contacts and magnets · USB-C on the case
LatencyAbout 3 ms budget · modeled for a well-sealed fit
