How it works

Four moves, one tiny earbud.

Spin the real v2 design, pull it apart, or follow the sound from the room to your eardrum.

1Seal

A foam tip blocks most outside sound, even with the power off.

2Listen

An outward mic hears the room.

3Soften

The audio chip turns down up to five bands, under a fixed ceiling.

4Play back

About 3 ms later it's in your ear, too fast to hear as an echo.

True-scale model of the v2 design. The ear comes from published anatomical dimensions, not a scan.

Smaller than it sounds.

Targets from the v2 design. Nothing here has been measured on a built earbud yet.

6 h+Battery target, topped up in the case
~3 msLatency budget for a sealed fit
5 bandsFilters you set, on the audio chip
